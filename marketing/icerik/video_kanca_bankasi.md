# Phase 0 Hook & Script Bank: 30 short videos (15 TR + 15 EN)

Product (brand "Lockglow", pending TÜRKPATENT and attorney check): lock-screen and home-screen English-vocabulary widgets. Turkish learners first, global learners second.
Phase 0 goal: measure demand with organic short video. The app is NOT available yet. Every CTA points to the early-access waitlist.
Created: 2026-10-11. Sources used: `veri/ornek_kelime_seti_25.json` (sample cards), `content-pipeline/plans/pilot_50.csv` (planned words), `raporlar/05_buyume_gelir_modeli.md` section 3.1 (format data).

## 0. Ground rules for every video

**Honesty (Phase 0)**
- Never say or show: "indir", "download", "App Store'da", "available now", store badges, ratings, user counts.
- Allowed CTA only: "link in bio → early access waitlist" (TR: "profildeki link → erken erişim listesi"). Wording may vary, meaning may not.
- Any mock widget or mock quiz is a design mockup. Keep a small corner tag on screen the whole time: TR "Konsept tasarım", EN "Concept mockup". Never screen-record a real app and never imply the mockup is a shipping product.
- Do not show or say "Lockglow" until the TÜRKPATENT search and the attorney's view are in. Scripts say "a lock-screen word widget" / "kilit ekranı kelime widget'ı".
- Reply script for "which app?" / "hangi uygulama?" comments: TR "Henüz yayında değil; erken erişim listesi profilde." / EN "Not released yet. Early access waitlist is in my bio."

**Accuracy**
- Every definition, Turkish gloss and false-friend claim comes from the sample cards (card ID given per video) or from the planned-word CSV hint plus plain common knowledge (marked "pilot word" below). Etymology appears only where the card has it with high confidence.
- Do not say "this comes up in the YDS/YÖKDİL/IELTS". Exam hashtags and the persona label are audience targeting only, not a claim about exam content. No exam statistics, no pass-rate or speed promises ("7 günde" style), no "guaranteed", no "never forget".
- No competitor names. No named tracks: use "no music" or "a trending sound from the platform's own library, low volume".
- Turkish: TDK spelling, correct ı/İ ş ğ ü ö ç, circumflex only where TDK requires it (e.g. "eski hâline").

**Hook rules**: the first 1.5 s must carry the on-screen text and (unless silent) the spoken line. Silent videos have no voice at all; the text carries the hook. First frame must already show the hook text (no logo intro, no "hey guys").

**Format note**: the research report's format mix (talking head 46%, app demo 31%, silent text overlay 16%, slideshow 6%; many top posts 6-9 s) is mirrored here, but "app demo" is replaced by mock-widget demos because there is no app yet (10% of this bank). Lengths run 7-22 s.

### TR-01 · "Aktüel" is not "actual"
- **Lang / format / persona / length:** TR · talking head · beginner · 14 s
- **Source:** card `en.actually.adv.1` (false friend: aktüel = güncel; actual = gerçek/asıl; "şu anda" = currently / at the moment)
- **HOOK (0-1.5 s):** on-screen "AKTÜEL ≠ ACTUAL" · spoken "'Actual', 'aktüel' demek değil!"
- **Alt hook (A/B):** on-screen "'Actual price' yazıyorsan bunu oku"
- **Script:**
  1. 0-1.5 Hook. Close-up, finger-wag at camera.
  2. 1.5-5 Say: "Türkçede 'aktüel', güncel demek. İngilizcede 'actual' ise gerçek, asıl demek." Text: aktüel = güncel · actual = gerçek, asıl.
  3. 5-8 Say: "'Şu anda' demek istiyorsan: 'currently' ya da 'at the moment'." Text: şu anda = currently / at the moment.
  4. 8-11.5 Split screen. Text: YANLIŞ "The actual price of petrol is very high." · DOĞRU "The current price of petrol is very high." Say: "Benzinin güncel fiyatı, 'current price'; 'actual' değil."
  5. 11.5-14 CTA (TR-A).
- **On-screen text, in order:** AKTÜEL ≠ ACTUAL · aktüel = güncel · actual = gerçek, asıl · şu anda = currently · YANLIŞ / DOĞRU pair · Link profilde → erken erişim listesi
- **CTA:** TR-A
- **Caption:** "Aktüel = güncel. Actual = gerçek. 'Şu anda' için: currently. Sen bu tuzağa düştün mü?" #ingilizce #ingilizcekelime #sahtedostlar #falsefriends #ingilizcedersi
- **Tests:** hook rate (3-s hold) on the strongest card-backed false friend; secondary: saves.
