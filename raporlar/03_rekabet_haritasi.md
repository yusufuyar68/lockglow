# Competitive landscape: iOS "vocabulary widget / learn a new word daily" apps
Reference app: **Widget Vocabulary: Learn Words** (Orbit Studios Teknoloji Ltd. Sti., id6775767149). Research date: 2026-10-10.

**How to read this report**
- All App Store facts (ratings, dates, sizes, IAP lists, chart badges, subtitles) were pulled live on 2026-10-10. Source tags are in the key at the end.
- Anything labelled "EST" is a third-party estimate. Those estimates disagree with each other by up to 14x, so treat revenue numbers as low confidence.
- I could not find a "Words: vocabulary builder" app by that exact name in the US, GB or IN storefronts. The nearest matches are WordUp, WordPal, HelloWords (HelloTalk) and "Words - Learn Languages Fast" (Andrey Lebedev).
- In the feature matrix, ✗ means "not advertised in the listing text". The feature may still exist.

---

## 0. Key findings

1. **Monkey Taps' "Vocabulary" is the category leader by a wide margin.**
   - 219,574 US ratings at 4.77 (328K worldwide per MWM). [L][MWM]
   - Released 2016, updated 2026-10-06, #26 in US Education. [P]
   - MWM puts it at 12.5M+ all-time downloads. [MWM]
   - Sensor Tower's public estimate is about 200k downloads and $700k revenue last month (US only). [ST]
   - It is one of 7 apps built on one shared engine.
2. **The reference app is charting in emerging storefronts.** [P][C]
   - #3 in Education in Turkey, #3 in Kazakhstan, #18 in Colombia, #21 in Vietnam, #38 in Chile, #51 in Japan.
   - It is not charting in the US (418 US ratings at 4.72; 190 TR ratings at 4.37).
   - Orbit's listing is English-only apart from localized subtitles in ES/PT/JA.
   - Monkey Taps does not localize its Vocabulary listing for TR, AR, VI, ID, TH, PL or RU.
3. **The widget-vocab niche is flooding with clones.**
   - My US search sample (1,472 unique apps, 16 queries) contains **86 vocab apps launched in 2026 that mention widgets or lock screen** (21 in 2025).
   - Only **3 of those 86 have reached 100 or more US ratings**. The median has 1 rating, and 39 have zero.
   - Launch cadence rose to roughly 7-11 per month from June to August 2026.
   - This is a lower bound, because search results are capped.
4. **Apple tightened Guideline 4.3 on 2026-06-08.** Apps in saturated categories can now be removed if they are not updated, not improved, or fail to attract customers. [MR][MN]
5. **Turkish-market vocab pricing is far below the reference app's.**
   - Local incumbent Kolay Ingilizce Kelime Ogren: ₺299.99/yr Pro and ₺499.99 lifetime. [P]
   - Orbit in TR: ₺499.99/week and ₺2,499.99/yr. [P]
   - Recent TR reviews of Orbit are 13 of 28 at 1-2 stars, mostly about price and the paywall. [reviews feed]
6. **Monkey Taps' own weak points** are visible in its recent US reviews. [reviews feed]
   - Forced annual billing after a trial.
   - A paywall after a long onboarding.
   - Hallucinated or incorrect words.
   - No category filter.
   - A privacy class-action investigation on its ad and tracking SDKs. [CLS]

## 2. Detailed profiles (top 8)

### 2.1 Monkey Taps - Vocabulary - Learn words daily
- **Position.** Category leader.
  - Rating: 219,574 US ratings at 4.77. [L]
  - Reach: 12.5M+ downloads (MWM) and 328K total ratings. [MWM]
  - Subtitle: "Word of the day widget & games". [P]
- **Current lead copy.** The product page opens with "The word of the day on your Lock Screen: learn a new word every unlock. 10,000+ English words by level and topic, with a widget and word games." [P] This is a shift toward lock-screen positioning, aimed straight at the clone wave.
- **Features** (per [P]/[L]): word of the day, difficulty levels, categories, themes, word games. Platforms are iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and iMessage. UI is localized into 6 languages (EN, FR, DE, IT, PT, ES). Minimum iOS is 18.0. Size is 206MB.
- **Localized names in the Vocabulary listing** [P]:
  - ES/PT/FR/DE/IT/JA/KO all have localized titles and subtitles.
  - MX, CO and AR use "Vocabulary - Aprende ingles".
  - TR, SA, IN, VN, ID, TH, PL and EG use the English title and "Write & speak more confidently".
- **Charts.** Top-20 in Education in CO (#7), AU (#10), BR (#12), ES (#13), IT (#13), AR (#11), CA (#15), MX (#18), FR (#19). Also US #26, GB #40, DE #58, IN #98. Not in TR top 200. [P]
- **TR footprint.** 1,202 TR ratings at 4.60, versus 219,574 in the US. Under 1% of its US ratings base. [L]
- **Pricing.** See section 4.
- **Differentiator.** Brand and ratings moat, a portfolio and bundle, Watch and iMessage extras, and a huge category and theme library.
- **Weaknesses** (recent 100 US reviews: 30 at 1 star, 13 at 2, 14 at 3, 13 at 4, 30 at 5) [reviews feed]:
  - Forced annual billing after a trial.
  - "Paywall required" after about 5 minutes of setup.
  - A word that does not exist (AI hallucination).
  - Wrong definitions.
  - The dislike button does nothing, so the same word repeats on the widget.
  - No control over categories.
  - Saved words are locked behind premium.

### 2.2 Orbit Studios - Widget Vocabulary: Learn Words (reference app)
- **Facts.**
  - Released 2026-06-24 and already at v2.2.5 (2026-09-25). [L]
  - 113MB, English UI, minimum iOS 15.0. [L]
  - Subtitle: "Easy & Fast English Learning". [P]
  - Developer site: orbitstudios.io/widget-vocabulary. [L]
- **Features from the listing** [L]: Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets, A1-C2 levels, flashcards (meaning, examples, pronunciation, related words), quizzes with "1,000+ levels", IELTS/TOEFL/GRE/SAT packs, multiple target languages (ES/FR/DE/IT/PT "and more"), native-speaker audio. No Watch app (platforms: phone and pad). [P]
- **Monetization.** Weekly $9.99 and yearly $49.99 (TR: ₺499.99 and ₺2,499.99). [P] The widget is gated by the paywall according to TR reviews.
- **Ratings.** US 418 at 4.72 [L]. TR 190 at 4.37 [L]. MWM puts global ratings at about 1.1K. [MWM]
- **Ratings by storefront** [L]: VN 240, TR 190, KZ 46, MX 39, JP 32, CO 23, BR 18.
- **Charts** [P]: TR #3, KZ #3, CO #18, VN #21, CL #38, JP #51, MX #70, AR #70, AE #101, RU #146, SA #153. Not charting in the US.
- **Estimates (EST).**
  - MWM: 100k+ downloads. [MWM]
  - AppGoblin: about 21K installs, 2.6K MAU, and a monthly install trend of +4.3K. [AG]
  - These two disagree. A ratings-based sanity check (1.1K ratings at roughly 1-2% rating rate) suggests 55K-110K downloads. That is my own heuristic, not a sourced figure.
  - AppGoblin lists Flutter, RevenueCat, Facebook, Google and OneSignal SDKs.
- **Developer playbook.** The same developer also ships Holy Bible Widget: Daily Verse (id6760676817), Along: Motivation & Support (id6782822001), and Rooster alarm clock (id6799883840). [P]
  - Holy Bible Widget: 2,049 US ratings at 4.75, #68 US Reference. [L][P]
  - This is the Monkey Taps pattern (same widget engine across niches).
- **TR reviews** (28 most recent TR reviews) [reviews feed]:
  - 15 are 5-star, 11 are 1-star, 2 are 2-star.
  - Complaints: hard paywall, "weekly 500 TL", "widgets unusable without paying", "words natives do not know", a refund request after a trial.
  - Praise: large word pool and a clean UI.

### 2.3 Vocabulous - Word of the Day・Vocabulary
- **Facts.**
  - 38,952 US ratings at 4.67, 10 UI languages, 188MB. [L]
  - Subtitle: "English Vocab Builder & Games". [P]
  - Claims 7M+ downloads (self-claimed in its listing). [L]
- **Features** [L]:
  - Home and Lock Screen widgets, daily quizzes with spaced repetition, hangman, crosswords, "Stop Saying" word collections.
  - Editor-picked words, illustrations, audio, streaks, SAT/GRE/IELTS/TOEFL positioning.
- **Ownership.** TechBullion (2025-02-20) reports the app was acquired by long-time user John Steuart, who formed Vocabulous US with an R&D center in Warsaw. [TB] Techleap's headline gives a sale price of about EUR 830k (page blocked; price taken from search summary). [TL]
- **Estimates.** MWM 5M+ downloads. [MWM] AppGoblin 2.9M installs, about 57K MAU, "<$10K" monthly revenue and 65% ads. [AG] AppGoblin lists AppsFlyer and Adapty.
- **Pricing.** See section 4. The IAP list spans $7.99 weekly to $99.99 annual.

### 2.4 Atlas - Vocabulary Builder by Atlas (Turkish developer)
- **Facts.** 12,498 US ratings and 8,599 TR ratings at 4.75-4.77, 209MB, 7 UI languages. [L]
  - US subtitle: "Daily Word of the Day Widget". [P]
  - TR/TH/VN subtitle: "Word of the Day, Flashcards". [P]
- **Features** [L]: proficiency test, levelled curriculum, idioms, phrasal verbs, business English and exam English (TOEFL/GRE/SAT/IELTS), personal word journal, 6 exercise types, AI tutor, spaced repetition, home-screen widgets and reminders, weekly global challenges.
- **Weakness.** No Lock Screen widget is advertised. It is not in the top 100 Education chart in any storefront I checked, despite thousands of ratings. [P]
- **Estimates.** MWM 500k+ downloads [MWM]. AppGoblin 1.9M installs, about 36K MAU, "<$10K" monthly revenue, 62% ads. [AG]
- **Differentiator.** The deepest curriculum among the widget-friendly apps.

### 2.5 WordUp - Vocabulary Builder (Geeks Ltd, London)

  - No widget is advertised.

- **EST.** MWM: 1M+ downloads. [MWM]
- **Takeaway.** Large installed base, stale for 21 months, no widget advertised, and very low prices.

### 2.8 Widget-native niche challengers: Lingo Widget (Oguz Doruk) and Hourly Kana/Hangul/Hanzi (Radish Retail)

  - 11 UI languages, about $34.99 yearly price point, $4.99 for 30 days.

## 6. Monkey Taps playbook

1. **One engine, many topics.**

   - A 2025 growth article describes widgets as a visual differentiator in ASO and ads with "high volume, low competition" keyword demand (qualitative, no figures). [NEO]

## 10. Risks

- **Saturation.** 86 widget-vocab launches in 2026 against 3 reaching 100 ratings. The leader has 12.5M+ downloads and is repositioning toward the lock screen.
- **Apple Guideline 4.3(b), updated 2026-06-08.** [APPLE-GL][MR][MN]
  - Rejects apps "indistinguishable from what's already widely available" and now allows removal of live apps in saturated categories that are not updated, not improved, or fail to attract customers.
  - Repeated low-effort submissions can lead to Developer Program removal.
  - Vocabulary is not on Apple's named list (dating, flashlight, wallpaper, simple timers, fortune telling), but the reasoning applies. Multiple near-identical listings from one developer are the highest-risk pattern.
  - Related guidelines: 4.1(a) Copycats, 4.2.6 template-generated apps, 2.3.7 metadata (no trademarked terms in keywords), 3.1.2(c) (describe the value before asking to subscribe).
- **Privacy and tracking.** Ad and analytics SDK footprints (Facebook, Google, Mixpanel, OneSignal) show up on Monkey Taps and Orbit. [AG] A privacy class-action investigation already names Monkey Taps. [CLS] Guideline 5.1.2 applies. [APPLE-GL]
- **Subscription friction.** Weekly plans with trials are the main source of one-star billing complaints for both Monkey Taps and Orbit. [reviews feed]
- **Turkish market.**
  - Lira depreciation (42.97 on 2026-01-01 to about 48.4 in early September per Trading Economics) means TRY price tiers need re-tiering. [FX]
  - Price sensitivity is high (Orbit TR reviews).
  - Shallow charts can overstate momentum.
- **Estimate reliability.** Sensor Tower, AppGoblin and MWM disagree by up to 14x. Do not size a plan on any single figure.
- **Content licensing.** Definition and example sources need license checks (I did not research specific licenses).
