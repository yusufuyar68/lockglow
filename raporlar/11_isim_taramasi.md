## Bottom line
- **Wordglance: do not use.** A live Android vocabulary app with home-screen widgets already has this exact name. It is made by a Turkish indie developer, so it sits in your core market. "GLANCE" is also registered by others in classes 9/38/41/42.
- **Wordwink: do not use.** `WORDWINKS` is a registered, renewed US trademark in class 9 for a mobile app that teaches language. `wordwink.com` is registered, and the `@wordwink` handles are taken on three platforms.
- **Lexpeek: the only viable one of the three.** I found no exact hit in any app store, trademark register or domain. The residual risk is a crowded "-peek" naming family in the same niche, and some taken handles.

## Blockers
1. **Wordglance:**
   - The exact name is already used by a vocabulary-with-widgets Android app by Glance Works (Turkey): [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nusret.wordglance).
   - GlanceWord (a Turkish developer's iOS English-vocabulary widget app) is a near-identical name for the same product in your launch market.
   - Prior `GLANCE` marks cover classes 9/38/41/42 (InMobi, Glance Networks, Reserv, Mobile Pixels), plus Turkish `glance` and `glanceaı` records.
2. **Wordwink:**
   - US `WORDWINKS` Reg 4482587 is live in class 9 for a language-teaching mobile app, and the app is on the iOS store.
   - `wordwink.com` is taken.
   - The same-niche YouTube channel and the `@wordwink` handles on three platforms are taken.
3. **Lexpeek (not blockers, but watch items):**
   - `PEEK` marks in 9/42 (EU/UK).
   - The "-peek" vocabulary-widget family (Wordpeek etc.).
   - Instagram `@lexpeek` and TikTok `@lexpeek` are held by others.

## Recommendation
- **File Lexpeek first**, if you must pick one of these three. Suggested order: TÜRKPATENT (home market), then EUIPO, UK, US (intent-to-use) and a Madrid extension. Classes 9 (downloadable app), 41 (language and vocabulary education) and 42 (online software).
- **Get a trademark attorney's opinion before filing**, specifically on `LEXPEEK` against the `PEEK` marks in 9/42 and the "-peek" vocabulary apps. Also consider whether the "Lex-" legal connotation matters for your brand.
- **Domains to register now:** `lexpeek.com`, `.app`, `.io`, `.co`, `.com.tr`, optionally `.dev`. Defensive: `getlexpeek.com`, `lexpeekapp.com`.
- **Handles to claim:** X `@lexpeek` and YouTube `@lexpeek` (both appear free). For Instagram and TikTok, where `@lexpeek` is taken, use `@lexpeekapp` or `@getlexpeek` (all appeared free on Instagram, TikTok, X and YouTube).
- If none of the three suits you after the attorney review, generate new candidates. Avoid "Glance", "Wink" and "Word-" compounds in this niche, and avoid `w` for a Turkish and Vietnamese audience.
