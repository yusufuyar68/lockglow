Sources:

**Claimed in the App Store description [V]** (the Google Play description is identical):
1. Learn words from the **Lock Screen**: new words appear automatically through the day, with no extra study session.
2. **Customizable Home Screen widgets** that auto-update with fresh words and definitions, with a choice of what to learn.
3. **Level selection from A1 to C2.**
4. **"Thousands" of flashcards** showing meaning, example sentences, real-life usage, pronunciations and related vocabulary.
5. **Quizzes with 1,000+ levels** and progress tracking.
6. **Exam vocabulary packs** for IELTS, TOEFL, GRE and SAT.
7. **Multiple languages**: Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese "and more".
8. **Audio pronunciations** by native speakers.
9. **Optional premium subscription**: Weekly Premium and Yearly Premium, said to unlock advanced features, premium vocabulary packs, unlimited access to learning content and additional customization.
10. Support email support@orbitstudios.io, plus privacy policy and Terms of Use (EULA) links.

**Additional claims from screenshots [V]:**
- "10,000+ words" (screenshot 3).
- "10+ languages" (screenshot 8). The screenshot only shows six: English, Italian, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese.
- A "For You" word feed with phonetic transcription and an audio button.
- Notification-style prompts ("Do you know what X means?").
- A level map with a START QUIZ button.
- A mascot book character.

**From release notes [V]:**
- **Custom decks:** up to 5, each with 50, 100, 1,000 or more words.
- Add, remove and organize words.
- Show custom-deck words on Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets.
- **Phonetic transcriptions** can now appear on widgets.

**From the developer's site feature cards [V]:**
- Home-screen widgets show words, translations and example sentences.
- "Smart Flashcards" with your own or ready-made decks and a **spaced-repetition** system.
- Audio pronunciation by native speakers.
- The App Store text does not mention spaced repetition or translations.

**From the privacy policy [V]:**
- Widget settings, learning preferences, saved words and quiz progress "may be stored locally".
- Content comes from the app's built-in vocabulary database.
- The app does not read personal content such as messages, photos or contacts.

**Gaps [I]:**
- The English UI and the "Easy & Fast English Learning" subtitle show it is mainly an English-vocabulary tool for non-native speakers. The other-language claim is secondary.
- The store listing is localized into ES, DE, PT-BR, JA, KO, ZH, RU, NL, SV and IT, e.g. subtitle "Aprende inglés fácil y rápido" (Spanish) and "Englische Wörter lernen" (German). The app UI is English only.
